Burger Sauce

Some times you might find yourself with a delicious burger but not so juicy, this recipe is made to solve exactly this!

This tangy burger sauce will take homemade hamburgers up a notch. It’s also great with chips and fries!

Ingredientes

  • 1 cup mayonnaise
  • ½ cup ketchup
  • ¼ cup prepared yellow mustard
  • 2 teaspoons dried minced onion
  • ¼ teaspoon dried minced garlic
  • ⅛ teaspoon white vinegar
  • hot sauce to taste
  • seasoned pepper to taste

Steps

  1. Gather all ingredients.
  2. Whisk together mayonnaise, ketchup, mustard, onion, garlic, and vinegar in a bowl.
  3. Season with hot sauce and seasoned pepper.
  4. Cover and chill at least 1 hour before serving.
  5. Serve and enjoy!
