Curry

Rice and Curry is a typical dish in Japan, easy and cheap to make, it's easy to see why it's so popular across the country and abroad.

This recipe uses "House Vermont Curry" as the roux but you are free to use whichever you prefer.

Ingredients

  • House Vermont Curry (whole package)
  • Cooked rice (500g)
  • Onion (2 units)
  • Potato (2 units)
  • Carrot (.5 unit)
  • Pepper (1 unit)
  • Chicken breast(300g)
  • Olive oil
  • Fukujinzuke(optional)

Obs: The quantities serve about 3 people.

Steps

  1. Cut the onions as you like. Then take a frying pan and add a drizzle of olive oil. Add the chopped onions to the pan.
  2. Now start cutting the pepper in cubes. As soon as you finish dicing it, turn on the stove on low heat to start cooking the onion.
  3. Now that the onion is frying, dice the chicken breast as evenly as you can. set the chicken aside and sprinkle some salt on it.
  4. Check for the onion, if it's a little translucent, add the pepper. While waiting start cutting the potatos and carrots the same size as you cut the chicken breast.
  5. If the pepper is soft, add the chicken breast.
  6. Let the chicken breast fry for a few minutes, until the juices released by the chicken collect in the pan, then add the potatos and carrots. Wait the water to evaporate completely.
  7. When the water is fully evaporated, take some water and add it yourself (approximately 800 mL) and let the potato and carrot cook until soft.
  8. When you find the potatos and carrots soft it's time to add the roux. Add it and mix until homogeneous.
